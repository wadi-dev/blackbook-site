Introductions between those with the mandate to execute.

A private network of senior operators, investors, and advisers in London. By referral only.

The Mechanism

Members declare two things: their structural capabilities, and their current mandates. When a mutual alignment emerges, introductions are curated manually. Both parties must explicitly opt-in before any identity is revealed.

Operational Boundaries

This is a matching layer, not a social platform. There are no public directories, no deal feeds, and no internal messaging. To satisfy institutional compliance requirements, all confirmed introductions immediately transition to your own corporate channels. We do not intermediate, advise on, or structure transactions.

Structural Discretion

Privacy is engineered into the architecture, not toggled in a settings menu. Your identity, your firm, and your search radius remain strictly confidential until you authorize a release. Vouching metrics are private, and declined introductions are resolved silently.

Membership

Access is strictly by referral. New members must be invited by an existing participant, whose reputation remains tied to the invitee for the duration of their tenure.
