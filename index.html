A private network of senior operators, investors and advisers in London. By referral only. There is no application.

What it is

Every member declares two things: what they can open, and what they need. When one person's give matches another person's ask, we make the introduction by hand.

Both sides agree before either name is released. If one declines, the other is told only that it did not proceed. Nobody is ever told they were turned down.

What it is not

  • Not a directory. You cannot browse for a name. Search returns the seat, the sector and how far away someone is, never who they are.
  • Not a deal platform. We vouch for the person, never the deal. Nothing here is an offer, an inducement, or advice on any investment.
  • Not a feed. There is no timeline, no engagement loop and no reason to open this daily. Most weeks it should be quiet.
  • Not a messaging app. Once an introduction is made the conversation moves to your own channels, because your compliance team requires it to.

What we never do

These are not settings. They do not have a switch, and there is no version of Blackbook London in which they are turned off.

  • Your name is never shown in search. Another member sees your seat, your sector and how far away you are. Nothing else, until you have agreed.
  • How strongly you vouch is never shown to them. The scale is your own record, and nobody is ever told how you rated them. The only thing that can ever travel is an invitation you chose to send.
  • A block is never disclosed. The person you blocked is not told, and a searcher is never shown who is hidden from them.
  • We never see a conversation between members. Once an introduction is made, we are out of the way.

Getting in

Membership is by referral. A member spends one of their five invitations on you, and their name stays attached to yours for as long as you are here. That is the whole vetting mechanism, and it is deliberate.

There is no waiting list, no application form and no way to buy your way in. If you have not been referred, there is nothing here for you to do.
