Introductions between those with the mandate to execute.

A private network of senior operators, investors, and advisers in London. By referral only.

How it works

Each member states two things: what they can open, and what they are looking for. Where the two meet, we make the introduction by hand. Both sides agree before either name is shared.

What it is not

Not a social platform. There is no directory, no deal feed and no messaging. Once an introduction is agreed it moves to your own channels. We do not intermediate, advise on or structure transactions.

Discretion

Your name, your firm and what you are looking for stay private until you agree to an introduction. How you rate other members is seen by nobody. A declined introduction is closed quietly, and the other side is not told.

Membership

By referral only. A member invites you, and their name stays attached to yours for as long as you are here.
